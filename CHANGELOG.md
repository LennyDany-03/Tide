# Changelog

Every release of the Tide Android app. The website's changelog page and the
in-app update notes are both read from this file, so write entries for the
people using the app.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)
and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). See
`TRIGGER.md` for how a new version is released.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.3] - 2026-09-15

### Fixed

- Logging in no longer says "no account" when your keyboard slips a space into your email address.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-09-15

### Fixed

- Tide 1.0.1 could stay stuck on the launch screen and never open. It now starts normally.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-09-15

### Added

- In-app updates: Tide checks for a new version, downloads it and installs it for you.
- Settings, App updates: check for a new version any time.
- Pause a habit for a trip or a sick week without breaking its streak.
- A to-do list beside your habits, with due dates, repeats and reminders that work offline.
- Home screen widgets for today's habits, to-dos, streaks, a habit dashboard, a heatmap and a weekly recap.
- Pick which habit a widget shows, straight from the home screen.

### Changed

- Settings now shows the version of Tide you have installed.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-15

### Added

- Habits you log with one swipe, a hold per unit, or a dial for minutes.
- Streaks that survive a missed day with streak freezes.
- History as a month calendar and a year grid, plus weekly insights.
- Five palettes: Midnight, Deep water, Ink, Blossom and Paper.
- Accounts with email or Google, synced across devices.
- Tide Pro for unlimited habits, full history and every palette.
